A Free Alternative to Zoho Books

Zoho Books is a good product with a pricing model that scales against you: per organisation, per user tier, and a free plan capped at a revenue threshold with a limited feature set. For a small firm the software cost grows precisely as the business grows.

The alternative argument is not that this project out-features Zoho. It does not, and pretending otherwise would waste your time. The argument is that for core bookkeeping, GST invoicing and stock, a local application covers the requirement at zero recurring cost.

Where a cloud ledger is genuinely better

Bank feeds, automated reconciliation, a client portal, mobile capture of receipts, and multi-entity consolidation are real advantages of a hosted product. If your workflow depends on any of them, pay for it — the hours saved exceed the subscription.

Where a local ledger wins

Data residency: your ledger is a file on your disk, not rows in a vendor's multi-tenant database. Continuity: no billing event can lock you out of your own history. Cost: no per-organisation or per-user charge, and no revenue threshold that flips you onto a paid plan.

  • No per-organisation pricing
  • No user-seat tiers
  • No revenue threshold that ends the free plan
  • Works offline; no dependence on vendor uptime

Feature comparison, plainly

Both do double-entry bookkeeping, GST invoicing, party ledgers and financial statements. Zoho adds bank feeds, automation, portals and a much wider app ecosystem. This project adds exact decimal arithmetic, a two-sided statement layout familiar to Tally users, full keyboard operation, and a database file you can open in any SQLite client.

A migration path that does not lose history

Export your chart of accounts and party balances from Zoho, enter opening balances in a new company here at the start of a financial year, and keep the old system read-only for historical reference. Cut over at a period boundary so no single period is split across two systems.

Questions

Is there a free plan limit like Zoho's revenue cap?

No. There is no revenue threshold, seat count or organisation limit.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Bank reconciliation is done from statements you import or enter manually.

Can I invoice in multiple currencies?

The current build is focused on single-base-currency GST billing.

Can I move my data out later?

Yes — SQLite file plus JSON backup export. There is no export paywall.
