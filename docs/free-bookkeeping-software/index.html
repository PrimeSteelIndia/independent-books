Bookkeeping

Free Bookkeeping Software With Real Double Entry

A lot of free bookkeeping tools are really expense trackers with categories. They produce a pie chart of spending, which is useful for a household and useless for a business that has to produce a balance sheet and a trial balance at year end.

This is a double-entry system. Every transaction is entered as debits and credits that must balance before they post, which is why the reports reconcile instead of drifting.

The records a bookkeeper actually keeps

A chart of accounts organised as groups and ledgers, a Day Book listing every voucher in date order, a Trial Balance, and the two statements everything else feeds: Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss. Journal vouchers handle adjustments, depreciation and opening balances.

  • Groups and ledgers with opening balances
  • Journal, payment, receipt and contra vouchers
  • Day Book in date order
  • Trial Balance after every posting
  • Two-sided Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss

Working with several clients

Bookkeepers usually keep a set of companies rather than one. There is no per-company licence here, so client A and client B are two companies in the same installation and neither costs anything extra. Each company is its own SQLite file, which keeps client data cleanly separated and makes handover a single file copy.

Why exact decimals matter for bookkeeping

Floating-point arithmetic cannot represent 0.10 exactly. Accumulate a few thousand such values and the balance sheet is off by fractions of a paisa, which an accountant then hides with a rounding plug. That plug is a small lie in a statutory document. Decimal and integer-minor-unit arithmetic removes the cause instead of masking it.

Personal and non-profit use

Nothing in the licence limits the software to commercial bookkeeping. It is used for small trusts and societies that need proper statements without a software budget, and by sole traders who want a balance sheet rather than a spreadsheet.

Questions

Is double-entry really necessary for a small concern?

If you need a defensible balance sheet or a trial balance, yes. Single-entry trackers cannot produce one reliably.

Can I keep multiple clients in one install?

Yes, unlimited companies, each its own database file.

Does it handle opening balances?

Yes, including the difference-in-opening-balances line so a migrated ledger starts correctly.

Is there a cloud sync for collaboration?

No cloud sync by design. Multi-user access is over your local network in LAN mode.
