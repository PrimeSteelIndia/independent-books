Free Accounting Software for Small Business

Small businesses are the segment most exposed to subscription accounting. The work is real but the volume is modest, so a per-month, per-seat price is hard to justify — and the moment cash flow tightens, the software holding your statutory records is the first thing you are tempted to cancel. That is a bad position to be in.

Independent Books is sized for one to twenty people who need proper books, not an enterprise finance stack. It runs on the PC you already have.

The jobs a small business actually needs

Raise an invoice and get paid. Track who owes you and who you owe. Know your stock position without a stock take. Produce a balance sheet your bank or auditor will accept. File GST without re-keying numbers into a separate tool.

All of that is in the default build. The gateway screen surfaces today's sales, total receivable from debtors, and the current period's profit, so the daily question — did we make money, and who owes us — is answered on launch.

What you avoid by not renting

A cloud ledger means your books live on a vendor's multi-tenant database. If the subscription lapses, access to your own history is what gets restricted first. Locally stored books remove that failure mode entirely: the file is on your disk, and the licence cannot be revoked.

  • One SQLite file holds the whole company — copy it to back up
  • No per-seat cost when you hire your second or third person
  • No multi-company fee if you run a second entity
  • No price rise at renewal, because there is no renewal

When a paid product is the better buy

Be honest about the trade. If you need payroll with statutory filings, deep bank-feed reconciliation across many accounts, or a mobile app your field staff uses to photograph bills, a paid product will save you time. This one wins where the requirement is accurate double-entry bookkeeping with GST and stock, on hardware you control.

A sensible first week

Install, create your company with the correct financial year start, and enter opening balances from your last audited or filed position. Post a week of real vouchers alongside your existing process before switching over. If the two agree, cut over at the start of the next month.

Questions

How many users and companies are allowed?

Unlimited. There is no seat or company licensing.

Does it work on a shared office PC?

Yes. It is a local application; Windows user accounts on the same machine are separate concerns from the books file.

Can my accountant open the file?

Yes — it is standard SQLite, and JSON backup export is available if they prefer a portable format.

What happens if the project is abandoned?

The source is MIT licensed and your data is a plain SQLite file. Neither depends on the project staying alive.
