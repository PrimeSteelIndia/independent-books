ERP

Free ERP Software for a Small Business, Honestly Scoped

"Free ERP" searches usually end in one of two places: an open source suite that needs a consultant to install, or a vendor's lead-capture page. A small business rarely needs an ERP. It needs the accounting core, invoicing and stock, working reliably.

This project is that core. It is not marketed as an ERP, because calling it one would set the wrong expectation about modules it does not have.

What the core covers

Financial accounting with groups and ledgers, GST-compliant sales and purchase billing, inventory at weighted average cost, receivables and payables by party, and the statutory statements at period end. For a trading business or a small service firm, that is the majority of daily operational need.

  • General ledger and trial balance
  • GST sales and purchase billing
  • Inventory with valuation
  • Party-wise receivables and payables
  • Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss

What a real ERP adds

Payroll with statutory filing, manufacturing with bills of material and routing, CRM, purchase requisitions and approval chains, multi-entity consolidation, and role-based access control across departments. Those are substantial systems. A business that needs them should budget for them.

The honest decision rule

If your bottleneck is bookkeeping accuracy, GST compliance and knowing your stock position, the core is enough and the recurring cost saving is real. If your bottleneck is coordination across departments, an ERP addresses a problem this does not attempt.

Growing past it

The data leaves cleanly: SQLite plus JSON backup export. That means a later migration to a paid suite is a data exercise, not a hostage negotiation — which is the strongest argument for starting here rather than signing a multi-year contract on day one.

Questions

Is this a full ERP?

No. It is an accounting and billing core. It does not include payroll, manufacturing or CRM.

Can it handle manufacturing with BOM?

No bill-of-materials or production routing.

Can I migrate to a paid ERP later?

Yes, the database and JSON backup are portable.

Does it support multiple branches?

Separate companies can be maintained, but there is no consolidated multi-branch reporting.
