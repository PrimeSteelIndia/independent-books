Invoicing

Free Invoice Software Without an Invoice Cap

Free invoicing tools almost always meter something: twenty invoices a month, three customers, or a watermark on the PDF until you upgrade. For a business invoicing forty times a month, "free" turns into a monthly bill at exactly the moment the workflow is established.

There is no invoice counter here. The software is the same product on invoice one and invoice ten thousand.

The invoice is the accounting entry

When you raise an invoice, the posting already exists: the party's debtor balance rises, the sales ledger is credited, and the tax ledgers carry CGST/SGST or IGST. There is no second step to reconcile billing against books, because there is only one step.

  • Party balance updates on acceptance
  • Tax split chosen from the party's state
  • HSN code and rate printed on the line item
  • Credit terms reflected in the receivable report

Formatting that passes an accountant's eye

The printed invoice carries the description, quantity, rate, amount, the tax breakdown and the grand total, with the two-sided convention Indian accountants expect. Print and preview are on Ctrl+P, and the date can be set with F2 for a back-dated bill without leaving the voucher.

For contractors and freelancers

If your invoicing is occasional and international, the relevant question is currency and tax treatment rather than throughput. The current build focuses on single-base-currency GST billing for Indian and offline small-business use; if you invoice in three currencies a month, a hosted tool will fit better.

What it does not do

No online payment links, no customer self-service portal, no automated dunning emails. It produces invoices and keeps books. Chasing payment stays a human activity, or an integration someone builds on top of the SQLite file.

Questions

Is there a monthly invoice limit?

No limit of any kind.

Can I email invoices from the app?

No. You print or save the invoice and send it with your own mail client.

Are invoice templates customisable?

Print layout is fixed in the current build; the source is MIT licensed if you need a variant.

Does it add a watermark or branding?

No. There is no vendor branding on your invoices.
