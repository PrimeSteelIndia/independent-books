Billing

Free Billing Software for Retail and Wholesale

Billing software is judged at the counter: how many seconds per bill, and does the day's total match the cash drawer. Speed comes from keyboard entry, and matching comes from the bill posting to a real ledger rather than to a separate sales log.

This build is designed so an experienced operator never leaves the home row — F8 opens a sales voucher, the party and item lookup are type-ahead, and Ctrl+A accepts.

Built for counter speed

The voucher form is the interface. Date with F2, sales with F8, purchase with F9, receipt with F6, and payment with F5. Lookups are incremental, so typing three characters of a party name narrows to it instead of scrolling a dropdown.

Because it is a local application, there is no round-trip latency when you add a line. Line entry is bounded by your typing speed, not by a network.

Stock that reconciles with the ledger

Stock items carry quantity, rate and HSN. Valuation uses weighted average cost, so the stock summary is a defensible number rather than a quantity count with a guessed value. Sales reduce stock; purchases increase it; the closing stock figure feeds the balance sheet.

  • Weighted average cost valuation
  • HSN and rate stored per item
  • Stock Summary report
  • Closing stock carried into the Balance Sheet

What shops usually need on day one

Create the company, add parties as ledgers under Sundry Debtors, add stock items with rates, and post a test bill. If the printed invoice looks right and the debtor balance moves, you are operational. Most of the setup cost is data entry, not configuration.

Portable and offline by design

The portable ZIP runs from a USB drive, which suits a shop that shares one machine across shifts or wants the billing system to travel with the books. LAN mode covers a second counter on the same local network without any cloud account.

Questions

Can I use it for a restaurant or a service business?

Yes, for service lines; there is no table or KOT layout — it is a standard voucher-based billing flow.

Does it print thermal receipts?

Invoice printing is standard print-preview; thermal output depends on your printer driver.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, entirely.

Can two counters share the same books?

Yes, in LAN mode over your local network.
