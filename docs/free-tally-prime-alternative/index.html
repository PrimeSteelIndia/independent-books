A Free Tally Prime Alternative That Keeps Your Muscle Memory

People do not stay on Tally because they love the interface. They stay because the interface is in their hands — F8 means sales, Alt+G goes anywhere, and an experienced operator can post a voucher without looking at the screen. Any replacement that ignores that is asking for a productivity drop the business will not accept.

Independent Books keeps the keys, the two-sided statement layout, and the Gateway-style menu, and drops the licence fee and the renewal cycle.

What carries over

Voucher entry by function key, the Gateway screen with Masters and Vouchers, Day Book, Stock Summary, Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss in the familiar left/right layout, and masters for ledgers, groups, parties and stock items. GST is calculated at voucher level with the intra-state and inter-state split.

  • F2 change date, Alt+F2 report period
  • F4 contra, F5 payment, F6 receipt, F7 journal, F8 sales, F9 purchase
  • Alt+G universal Go To, Ctrl+A accept, Ctrl+P print
  • Two-sided Balance Sheet and P&L, not a stacked single column

What genuinely differs

Tally is a mature commercial product with statutory updates, an ecosystem of consultants, and modules this project does not attempt: payroll, statutory audit formats, multi-branch consolidation, and a large partner network. If you depend on any of those, that dependency is a real reason to pay.

The differences that favour this project are structural rather than cosmetic: the data is a standard SQLite file rather than a proprietary binary, the source is auditable, and the cost does not recur.

Moving your existing company across

Export from Tally as XML and import in Independent Books with Import → Import XML. Preview the counts, commit, then reconcile closing balances against a Tally trial balance for the same date before you rely on the new system for filing. Follow our step-by-step Tally Prime XML import tutorial for detailed instructions.

The honest test is one financial year, not one voucher. Import a completed year and compare the final balance sheet line by line.

Cost over five years

A Tally Prime silver plan plus the renewals an active business typically pays runs into six figures in rupees over five years. The equivalent spend here is zero, and the version you are running does not stop working when a renewal lapses.

Questions

Can it open Tally data directly?

Not the proprietary binary. Use the XML export path from Tally and the built-in XML importer.

Are the shortcuts identical?

The core voucher and navigation keys match Tally Prime. Some deep configuration screens do not have a one-to-one equivalent.

Does it support multi-currency?

The current build focuses on single-base-currency GST bookkeeping.

Is there a Mac version?

Yes — a DMG is published alongside the Windows builds, with the same keyboard shortcuts.
