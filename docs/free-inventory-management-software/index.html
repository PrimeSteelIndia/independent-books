Inventory

Free Inventory Software That Reconciles With Your Books

Standalone stock software and accounting software usually disagree, because quantity lives in one system and value lives in another. The month-end job becomes reconciling two numbers that should have been one.

Here, stock is part of the ledger. A sales voucher reduces quantity and posts revenue in the same posting, so the Stock Summary and the Balance Sheet's closing stock figure come from the same transaction.

What is tracked

Items carry a name, unit, HSN code and rate. Quantity moves on sales and purchases. Valuation uses weighted average cost, which is the method most Indian small businesses and their auditors expect for routine stock.

  • Stock items with unit and HSN
  • Weighted average cost valuation
  • Stock Summary report
  • Closing stock feeding the Balance Sheet

Why weighted average rather than FIFO

Weighted average is simple to audit: the value is total cost divided by total quantity, and any accountant can recompute it from the purchase register. FIFO layers are more precise and considerably harder to verify by hand, which matters when a small firm's auditor is checking the number rather than a system.

What this is not

It is not a warehouse management system. There is no bin location, barcode scanning, multi-warehouse transfer or pick-and-pack workflow. If your stock sits in one or two godowns and the question is "what is it worth and how many are left", it fits. If you run a fulfilment centre, it does not.

Scope for larger needs

Because the database is plain SQLite and the source is MIT licensed, an integrator can add warehouse dimensions or a barcode front end without asking permission. That is the intended escape hatch for requirements beyond the core.
