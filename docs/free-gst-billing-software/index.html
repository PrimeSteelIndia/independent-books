GST

Free GST Billing Software With the Split Done For You

The most common GST billing error in small businesses is not a rate error, it is a place-of-supply error: charging CGST plus SGST on an inter-state sale, or IGST on a local one. The invoice looks fine and the return is wrong.

Independent Books keys the tax treatment off the party's state. Pick an intra-state party and the voucher shows CGST and SGST at half the rate each. Pick an inter-state party and it shows IGST at the full rate. You cannot accidentally apply the wrong split.

The arithmetic, shown

A ₹50,000 taxable sale at 18% to a party in the same state: ₹4,500 CGST plus ₹4,500 SGST, grand total ₹59,000. The same sale to a party in another state: ₹9,000 IGST, grand total ₹59,000. The total is identical because the rate is identical — only the distribution changes, and that distribution is what the return cares about.

Amounts are computed with exact decimal arithmetic, so the tax lines add to the invoice total exactly, with no one-paisa balancing plug.

What a billing workflow needs beyond tax

An invoice has to print cleanly, carry the HSN code and rate, respect the party's credit terms, and post to the right ledger so the debtor balance is correct the moment you accept it. All four happen in one screen at voucher entry rather than in a separate billing tool.

  • HSN codes stored on stock items
  • Automatic tax ledger postings
  • Immediate debtor balance update
  • Invoice preview and print with Ctrl+P
  • Date change for back-dated vouchers with F2

Offline billing is a feature, not a limitation

A counter that cannot raise a bill because the internet is down is a business that has stopped. Because the application serves from 127.0.0.1, invoice generation has no network dependency at all. It works during an outage, in a basement, and on a laptop at a client site.

Returns, credit notes and corrections

Credit notes and debit notes adjust the party ledger through the standard voucher types, and because every voucher is double-entry, a correction propagates to the trial balance rather than sitting in a billing-only log that never reconciles to your books.

Questions

Does it file GST returns for me?

No. It maintains accurate books and invoices; GST portal filing remains with you or your tax professional.

Does it support multiple GST rates?

Yes — rates are set per stock item or ledger as required.

Can it bill without an internet connection?

Yes. Everything runs locally on your machine.

Is e-invoicing supported?

No. The build focuses on invoice generation and statutory bookkeeping.
