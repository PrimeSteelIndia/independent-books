Free Accounting Software in India, Built Around GST

Indian accounting software has two costs most buyers forget. The first is the licence. The second is the annual "upgrade" that is functionally mandatory because statutory formats change and your old version stops being supported. Independent Books removes both, and it treats GST as a first-class part of the voucher rather than an add-on module.

When you raise a sales voucher, the party's state decides the tax treatment. Same state gets CGST and SGST at half the rate each. A different state gets IGST at the full rate. The voucher previews the split before you accept it, so a wrong-state invoice is caught at entry rather than at filing.

GST handling that matches how invoices are actually checked

An 18% sale of ₹50,000 to a party in your own state shows ₹4,500 CGST and ₹4,500 SGST, totalling ₹59,000. The same sale to a party in another state shows ₹9,000 IGST. Both totals reconcile exactly, and the tax ledgers post on the correct side of the trial balance.

Stock items carry HSN codes and rates, so the description line on the printed invoice is the one your buyer's accountant expects to see.

Migrating off Tally without re-keying history

The realistic blocker to switching is not features, it is six years of vouchers. Export masters and vouchers from Tally as XML, then use Import → Import XML in Independent Books. The import previews transaction counts before you commit, so you can confirm the voucher count matches the source before the ledger moves.

This is the single most useful thing to test before you commit to a switch: import one financial year and check the closing balances against your old system.

Keyboard-first, because volume is the real cost

An accountant posting 300 invoices a day cannot afford a mouse. The familiar function keys are preserved: F2 changes date, F4 contra, F5 payment, F6 receipt, F7 journal, F8 sales, F9 purchase, F10 for the remaining voucher types, Alt+G to jump anywhere, Alt+F2 for the report period, Ctrl+A to accept.

For staff trained on Tally, the muscle memory transfers on day one. That is usually the difference between a switch that sticks and one that is abandoned in a week.

Practical details Indian buyers ask about

The data file is a normal SQLite database, so an accountant or auditor can inspect it with standard tools, and backup is copying one file. It runs on Windows 10 and 11, on macOS, and on Linux. For a shop floor or a partner who works from a tablet, LAN mode serves the interface over your local Wi-Fi so several people can post to the same books without a cloud subscription.

Questions

Does it calculate GST automatically?

Yes. CGST and SGST for intra-state supply, IGST for inter-state, applied from the party's state at voucher entry.

Can it print a GST-compliant invoice?

Yes — invoice preview and print are built in via Ctrl+P.

Is e-invoicing or e-way bill generation included?

No. It produces the invoice and the ledger; portal filing stays with your tax professional or the government portal.

Can multiple people use it in one office?

Yes, in LAN mode over your local network. No internet or cloud account involved.
