Head to head

Zoho Books vs Independent Books

These two products solve the same core problem from opposite directions. Zoho Books optimises for convenience and integration; Independent Books optimises for ownership and cost. Neither position is universally correct.

Pricing model

Zoho Books prices per organisation and per user tier, with a free plan bounded by revenue and features. Independent Books has no tiers: no organisation limit, no user seats, no revenue cap, no upgrade path to buy.

Where your data sits

Zoho holds your books in its cloud; you access them while the subscription is active. Independent Books keeps a SQLite file on your machine. The practical difference appears in three situations: an outage, a lapsed payment, and an auditor who wants the raw records.

  • Offline access: local only vs internet required
  • Continuity: file on disk vs account access
  • Raw data: SQLite vs API export

Automation and integrations

This is Zoho's strongest ground and the honest reason many businesses should choose it. Bank feeds, automated reconciliation, payment reminders, a client portal, and integration with the wider Zoho suite are capabilities that take real work to replicate. If your month-end depends on them, the subscription is buying back your time.

Bookkeeping core and GST

Both produce double-entry books with GST invoicing, party ledgers and financial statements. Independent Books adds exact decimal arithmetic, a two-sided statement layout that Tally-trained accountants expect, full keyboard operation, and a printable invoice without a vendor watermark.

Choosing between them

Choose Zoho if you want zero maintenance, bank automation, and access from anywhere on any device. Choose Independent Books if your requirement is accurate GST bookkeeping on hardware you control, at no recurring cost, with data you can hand to an auditor as a file.

Questions

Can I export from Zoho into Independent Books?

Export your chart of accounts and party balances, then enter opening balances at a financial-year boundary.

Does Independent Books have an API?

The local backend serves the interface; there is no published public API contract.

Which is better for a team working remotely?

Zoho Books, because it is hosted. Independent Books is designed for local and LAN use.

Is there a revenue limit on the free version?

No. There is no free tier — the whole product is free.
